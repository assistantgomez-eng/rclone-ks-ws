Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

This app is used only by its owner. It accesses only the Google Drive files that it creates itself (the drive.file scope), to store the owner's encrypted backups.

No data is collected, shared, or sold to anyone. No personal information about other people is processed.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings (Third-party apps with account access, https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
